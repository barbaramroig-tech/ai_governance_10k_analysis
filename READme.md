# AI Governance,Legal Risk, and Financial Materiality in Public Company 10-K Disclosures

## Overview
This project analyzes how public technology companies disclose:
1. AI risks
2. Legal liability
3. Privacy concerns
4. Intellectual property risks
5. Regulatory risks
6. AI governance practices

### Research Questions
- How do public companies describe AI-related risks?
- What governance mechanisms are disclosed?
- How do companies discuss legal liability arising from AI?
- What patterns emerge across firms?

## Companies
* Microsoft
* Alphabet (Google)
* Meta
* Amazon
* Palantir

## Why These Companies?
These companies represent different AI business models and governance challenges:
- Microsoft: Enterprise AI and OpenAI partnership
- Alphabet: Search, cloud computing, and foundation models
- Meta: Open-source AI and social media platforms
- Amazon: Cloud infrastructure, generative AI, and e-commerce
- Palantir: Government, defense, and enterprise AI

### Financial Questions
* How important is AI to the company's future growth strategy?
* Does the company discuss significant investments in AI infrastructure?
* Does the company identify AI as a material business opportunity?
* Does the company identify AI as a source of financial, legal, or regulatory risk?
* How do companies balance AI opportunity with AI-related costs and liabilities?
